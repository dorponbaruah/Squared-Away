How to Play

Take turns dragging between two neighboring dots to draw one line — up, down, left or right, never diagonal. Watch out: if you draw the third side of a box, you're handing it to the next player. Complete a box and it's claimed with your initial, plus you go again — chain several in one turn if you can. When every line is drawn, whoever claimed the most boxes wins.

Power boxes glow before anyone claims them. Finish one and you trigger its bonus:
🟡 Gold — that box scores double.
🔷 Sapphire — banks one extra move for later in your turn.

VS AI: tap ⓘ on an opponent's card to read how they play.

If the turn timer is on and it runs out, you're eliminated — the game continues without you.
